DESIGN AND CONTENT DECISIONS
- Midnight navy, warm ivory and a restrained brass accent; disciplined typography.
- One clear hero and a fictional adult sport-shooting athlete image.
- Purposeful sequence: discipline selection, academy mindset, training journey,
  branch discovery, notices, FAQs and next action.
- Real server-rendered pages and persistent form submissions, with an operational
  inbox behind authentication.
- Clear initial branch placeholders in admin only, ready to expand.
- No borrowed medals, testimonials, affiliations, age limits or fee claims.
- Self-hosted fonts and images; no third-party tracking scripts or font requests.

The supplied 11-page blueprint was used for academy naming, the expandable six-
branch model, role roadmap, public sitemap, enquiry/demo distinction and the rule
that website submissions must not activate student accounts. The user narrowed
this first implementation to superadmin, public website and its CMS.


PUBLIC WEBSITE REDESIGN — 24 OCTOBER 2026
Additional references explicitly requested by the user:
https://prime10xshootingrange.com/
https://gurudronacharyashootingacademy.com/

Reviewed both homepages and their visible navigation, trial/enquiry calls to action,
training descriptions and galleries. The redesign prioritises plain language and
a direct explanation of air rifle / air pistol training. It replaces the original
condensed all-caps typography, slogan-heavy copy, target illustrations and generated
hero image with readable sentence-case headings, real sports photography, clear
program cards, a homepage callback form and a three-step introduction to joining.

Four real Creative Commons reference images are saved locally, offered in every CMS
image selector, and attributed publicly at /photo-credits. They are identified as
reference photographs; no athlete or venue is presented as affiliated with ARSA.
Original source URLs, licences and download metadata are in docs/photo-sources.json.
All active section photos can be replaced through Homepage, Programs and Gallery
inside the Website CMS. The previous source and database snapshot are preserved
in tmp/before-redesign. Administrator credentials and existing enquiries are kept.
